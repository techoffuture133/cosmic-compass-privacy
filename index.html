Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Cosmic Compass: Tarot and Horoscope is a paid application available as a one-time purchase on the Google Play Store. The app does not contain advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected.

2. Google Sign-In Authentication

The app uses “Sign in with Google” for user authentication.

When you sign in, the app may access:

  • Email address
  • Name (first and last)
  • Profile picture
  • Google User ID

This information is used only for authentication and basic personalization within the app experience. Authentication is securely handled through Google Sign-In. The developer does not intentionally store personal Google account data on external servers.

For more information about Google’s privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Collection

The app does not collect, track, sell, or analyze personal data for advertising or analytics purposes.

No analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking tools, or third-party marketing services are used.

User preferences (such as zodiac sign, birth date, or app settings) may be stored locally on the user’s device to improve the app experience.

4. Data Retention

No personal data is intentionally stored by the developer outside the active app experience.

Locally stored preferences remain on the device until:

  • the user clears app data, or
  • the app is uninstalled.

5. Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Google Sign-In data handling is governed solely by Google’s own privacy policy.

6. Payments

All payments are processed exclusively through Google Play. The app does not access, process, or store payment or billing information.

7. User Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Users may revoke Google account access at any time through their Google Account permissions.

Because the app does not maintain external user databases, deleting the app or logging out removes locally stored session data.

8. Children’s Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app and/or this page.
