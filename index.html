Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Cosmic Compass: Horoscope ("the App") is a mobile application available on the Google Play Store. The App is designed for entertainment and personal reflection and does not require account creation, login, or authentication.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and how user privacy is protected.

2. No Account or Registration

The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information. All core functionality is available without registration or identity verification.

3. Data Storage and Local Processing

The App stores certain information locally on the user's device in order to provide functionality, such as:

  • selected zodiac sign
  • optional birth date or preferences entered by the user
  • app settings and personalization choices
  • locally generated readings, card draws, and history

This information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer or external servers for analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes. The developer does not have access to data stored locally on user devices.

4. No Analytics or Tracking

The App does NOT collect, track, analyze, or share personal or behavioral data.

The App does not use:

  • analytics services
  • advertising SDKs
  • tracking pixels or cookies
  • user profiling or behavioral monitoring tools

5. Data Control

Because all information is stored locally, users have full control over their data. Data can be removed at any time by:

  • clearing app data in device settings, or
  • uninstalling and reinstalling the App.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party services for analytics, advertising, social login, or data collection.

Any external links (if present) are opened at the user's discretion and are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Payments

If the App is distributed through Google Play, payments (if applicable) are processed exclusively by Google Play. The developer does not access, process, or store payment or billing information.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Updates will be posted within the App and/or on the official privacy policy page with an updated "Last updated" date.
